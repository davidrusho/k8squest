Change only the hostPath `path` of the volume named `etcd-data` in `/etc/kubernetes/manifests/etcd.yaml` to `/var/lib/etcd-restore`; the kubelet recreates the etcd Pod by itself. Leave `--data-dir` and the `mountPath` alone: inside the container etcd still sees its data at `/var/lib/etcd`. On a kubeadm cluster also set `etcd.local.dataDir` in the `kubeadm-config` ConfigMap (`kubectl -n kube-system edit cm kubeadm-config`), after the restart, because that ConfigMap is part of the restored data. Otherwise the next `kubeadm upgrade` writes an etcd manifest for the old directory.
