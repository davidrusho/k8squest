A NotReady node means its kubelet stopped reporting. `kubectl describe node node01` shows the condition and its reason; the cause is on the node: `systemctl status kubelet` and `journalctl -u kubelet -n 100 --no-pager`. Here the log says the kubelet cannot load its configuration file, because the `--config` path in the systemd drop-in is wrong. Fix the path where it is set, run `systemctl daemon-reload` and restart the kubelet.
