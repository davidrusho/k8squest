🔍 HINT 1: Start from the documentation's order

   limactl shell k8squest-cp

The kubeadm page "Creating a cluster with kubeadm" has the order: choose a pod
network add-on FIRST, because it can require an argument to kubeadm init. Flannel's
page says what:

   "pass --pod-network-cidr=10.244.0.0/16 to kubeadm init to ensure that the podCIDR is set"

After kubeadm init finishes it prints what to do next. Read that output; it is the
recipe for the kubeconfig and for the join command you will need in level 73.

🤔 Question: what would happen to Flannel if the node had no podCIDR?
