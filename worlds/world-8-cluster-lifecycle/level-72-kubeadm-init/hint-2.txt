🔍 HINT 2: kubectl, then the node's state

kubeadm writes the administrator's kubeconfig to /etc/kubernetes/admin.conf and
tells you to copy it to $HOME/.kube/config. Do that, then:

   kubectl get nodes
   kubectl get pods -A
   kubectl describe node lima-k8squest-cp | grep -A3 Ready

The node is NotReady and CoreDNS is Pending. The Ready condition says why: there is
no CNI configuration yet. A pod network add-on supplies it. kubeadm is CNI agnostic:
installing the add-on is your job, with kubectl apply.
