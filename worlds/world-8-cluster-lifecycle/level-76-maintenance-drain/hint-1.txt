🔍 HINT 1: See what runs where

   limactl shell k8squest-cp
   kubectl get nodes
   kubectl get pods -A -o wide
   kubectl get pdb -n k8squest

Two things on the worker will stop a plain drain: pods of a DaemonSet (Flannel and
kube-proxy run on every node) and pods with an emptyDir volume (their data is lost
when the pod is evicted). A third thing paces the drain: the PodDisruptionBudget.

   kubectl drain lima-k8squest-worker

Read the error. It names the flag for each obstacle. (A drain that fails has already
cordoned the node: look at kubectl get nodes.)
