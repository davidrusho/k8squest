🔍 HINT 2: The flags, and what the budget does

   kubectl drain lima-k8squest-worker --ignore-daemonsets --delete-emptydir-data

drain cordons the node first (it shows Ready,SchedulingDisabled), then evicts the pods
through the Eviction API, which respects the PodDisruptionBudget. With 4 replicas and
minAvailable: 3, one pod may be down at a time. You will see lines like:

   error when evicting pods/"web-..." (will retry after 5s): Cannot evict pod as it would violate the pod's disruption budget.

That is not a failure. The Deployment has already created a replacement on the
control plane, and as soon as it is Ready the next eviction is allowed. Wait; do not
delete the PodDisruptionBudget and do not force-delete pods.
