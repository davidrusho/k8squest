🔍 HINT 1: Observing the Problem

1️⃣ Is the route attached? Read its status:
   kubectl get httproute -n k8squest
   kubectl describe httproute echo-route -n k8squest
   Look at Status > Parents > Conditions (Accepted, ResolvedRefs).
   An empty status is itself a clue: nothing has claimed the route.

2️⃣ Which Gateways exist, and what are they called?
   kubectl get gateway -n k8squest
   kubectl get gatewayclass

3️⃣ And the backend:
   kubectl get svc echo -n k8squest

🤔 Questions:
• Does the route's parentRefs name match a Gateway?
• Does the route's backend port match the Service?
