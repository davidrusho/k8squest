🔍 HINT 1: Observing the Problem

1️⃣ Look at the pods:
   kubectl get pods -n k8squest
   kubectl describe pod <one-of-the-new-pods> -n k8squest
   What does the Events section say about the image?

   helm list -n k8squest
   helm history webapp -n k8squest

3️⃣ How many revisions are there, and what does each one say?

🤔 Questions:
• Which revision was the last good one?
• What changed between revision 1 and revision 2?
  (helm get values webapp -n k8squest --all --revision 1, then --revision 2)
