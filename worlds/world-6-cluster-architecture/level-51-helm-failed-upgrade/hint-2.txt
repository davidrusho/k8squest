🎯 HINT 2: Two Ways Out

Helm keeps every revision of a release, so you can go back or go forward:

• Go back:    helm rollback <RELEASE> [REVISION]
• Go forward: helm upgrade <RELEASE> <CHART> with corrected values

Editing the Deployment directly (kubectl edit / set image) is a trap: the
pods may recover, but Helm still believes revision 2 is what is deployed.

💡 Check which revision has the good image tag before you pick one.
