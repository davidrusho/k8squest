🎯 HINT 2: How a patch finds its target

In kustomization.yaml, each entry under `patches:` has a `path` (the patch)
and an optional `target` (which resources to patch). The target selects
resources by group, version, kind, name, namespace and label/annotation selectors.

If the target matches nothing, Kustomize does not complain. The patch is skipped.

💡 Look at kustomize/overlays/prod/kustomization.yaml in
   $HOME/.k8squest/work/level-52-kustomize-overlay and compare `target.name` with
   `metadata.name` in kustomize/base/deployment.yaml.
