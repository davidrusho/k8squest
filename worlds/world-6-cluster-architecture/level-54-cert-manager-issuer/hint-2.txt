🎯 HINT 2: Issuer versus ClusterIssuer

A Certificate picks its issuer through spec.issuerRef (name, kind, group).

• Issuer         is namespaced: only Certificates in the same namespace can use it.
• ClusterIssuer  is cluster-wide: usable from any namespace.

The default kind is Issuer. Compare:
   kubectl get certificate web-cert -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.issuerRef}'
with the objects from `kubectl get issuer,clusterissuer -A`.
