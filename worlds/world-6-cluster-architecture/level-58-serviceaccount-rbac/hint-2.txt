🎯 HINT 2: A RoleBinding names its subjects exactly

A RoleBinding connects a roleRef (the permissions) to subjects (the identities).
A ServiceAccount subject is identified by BOTH name and namespace:

   subjects:
   - kind: ServiceAccount
     name: reports-sa
     namespace: <the namespace where the ServiceAccount lives>

system:serviceaccount:default:reports-sa and
system:serviceaccount:k8squest:reports-sa are two different identities.

💡 Read the binding:
   kubectl get rolebinding reports-binding -n k8squest -o yaml
