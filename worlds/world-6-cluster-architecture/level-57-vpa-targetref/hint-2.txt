🎯 HINT 2: How a VPA finds its pods

A VPA does not select pods with labels. spec.targetRef points at a
controller (Deployment, StatefulSet, ...) by apiVersion, kind and name, and
the VPA takes that controller's pod selector.

If the targetRef cannot be resolved, the VPA cannot match any pods, so the
recommender has nothing to measure and status.recommendation stays empty.

💡 Compare targetRef.name with `kubectl get deployment -n k8squest`.
   Keep updateMode: "Off" (recommend only).
