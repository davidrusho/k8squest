🔍 HINT 1: Observing the Problem

1️⃣ Does the VPA say anything?
   kubectl get vpa -n k8squest
   kubectl describe vpa hamster-vpa -n k8squest
   Look at Status > Conditions.

2️⃣ Are the pods being measured?
   kubectl get deployment,pods -n k8squest
   kubectl top pods -n k8squest

3️⃣ What does the VPA think it is targeting?
   kubectl get vpa hamster-vpa -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.targetRef}'
