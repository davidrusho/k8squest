🎯 HINT 2: Consent comes from the target

A route may not use a backend in another namespace unless the owner of the
backend says so. That consent is a ReferenceGrant:

• it lives in the namespace of the thing being referenced (the Service)
• `from` lists who may reference it: group, kind, and namespace of the referrer
• `to` lists what may be referenced: group, kind (and optionally a name)

Compare `from.namespace` in the existing grant with the namespace that
holds shop-route.
