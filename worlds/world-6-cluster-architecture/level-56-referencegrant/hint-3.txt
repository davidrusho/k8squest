✅ HINT 3: Complete Solution

Change `from.namespace` from `default` to `k8squest`:

   apiVersion: gateway.networking.k8s.io/v1beta1
   kind: ReferenceGrant
   metadata:
     name: allow-routes
     namespace: k8squest-backend
   spec:
     from:
     - group: gateway.networking.k8s.io
       kind: HTTPRoute
       namespace: k8squest
     to:
     - group: ""
       kind: Service

   kubectl apply -f solution.yaml
   kubectl get httproute shop-route -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.status.parents[0].conditions}'
