🔍 HINT 2: Go to the Node

The Ready condition is reported by the kubelet. The node controller adds the
node.kubernetes.io/unreachable taint when the reports stop, and the scheduler
will not place pods on a tainted node.

A kind node is a Docker container. Open a shell in it:

   docker exec -it k8squest-multinode-worker bash

Then look at the service:

   systemctl status kubelet
   systemctl is-enabled kubelet
   journalctl -u kubelet --no-pager -n 20

🤔 Question: the unit is stopped. Is it also set to start at boot?
