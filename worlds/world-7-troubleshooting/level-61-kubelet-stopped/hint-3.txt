🔍 HINT 3: The Fix

Inside the node (docker exec -it k8squest-multinode-worker bash):

   systemctl enable --now kubelet

`enable` makes it start at boot, `--now` also starts it right away.

Then, from your own terminal:

   kubectl get nodes
   kubectl get pods -n k8squest -o wide

The node turns Ready within seconds and the taint disappears; the Pending pods
are scheduled on their own.
