🔍 HINT 3: The Fix

The application reads DB_HOST. The ConfigMap defines DB_HOTS. Rename the key:

   kubectl edit configmap invoice-config -n k8squest

Environment variables are read when a container starts, so restart the pods to
make them pick up the change (a container waiting in CrashLoopBackOff may take up
to five minutes to retry on its own):

   kubectl rollout restart deployment/invoice-api -n k8squest
   kubectl get pods -n k8squest

Do not delete the log-shipper container: it is part of the pod.
