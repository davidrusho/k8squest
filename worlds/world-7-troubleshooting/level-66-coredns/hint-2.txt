🔍 HINT 2: Two Things to Check

A. Is anything answering? Look at the CoreDNS Deployment: how many replicas are
   ready? A client with no DNS server to talk to times out.

B. Once something answers, is it serving the right domain? Read its configuration:
   kubectl get configmap coredns -n kube-system -o yaml

   The line that starts with `kubernetes` names the zone it is authoritative for.
   What is this cluster's domain? (kubectl exec ... cat /etc/resolv.conf shows the search path.)

🤔 Question: after you scale CoreDNS up, does the lookup work? If not, B is your answer.
