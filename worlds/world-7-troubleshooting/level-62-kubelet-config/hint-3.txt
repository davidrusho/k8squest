🔍 HINT 3: The Fix

The kubelet configuration points authentication.x509.clientCAFile at
/etc/kubernetes/pki/ca.crtt. Correct it (the file is /etc/kubernetes/pki/ca.crt):

   vi /var/lib/kubelet/config.yaml
   systemctl restart kubelet
   systemctl status kubelet

Then from your own terminal:

   kubectl get nodes
   kubectl get pods -n k8squest -o wide
