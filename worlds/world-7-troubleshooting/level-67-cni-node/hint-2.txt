🔍 HINT 2: Follow the CNI

The message is "cni plugin not initialized". On kind, the CNI plugin (kindnet) runs
as a DaemonSet in kube-system and writes each node's CNI configuration when its pod
starts.

   kubectl get ds -n kube-system
   kubectl get pods -n kube-system -l app=kindnet -o wide

Compare DESIRED with the number of nodes, and look for the node that has no kindnet pod.
Then ask why the DaemonSet skips it:

   kubectl get ds kindnet -n kube-system -o yaml      # spec.template.spec.nodeSelector
   kubectl get nodes --show-labels
