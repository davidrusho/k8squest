🔍 HINT 3: The Fix

The kindnet DaemonSet only runs on nodes labelled k8squest.io/cni=true. worker2
lacks the label. Either give it the label:

   kubectl label node k8squest-multinode-worker2 k8squest.io/cni=true

or remove the nodeSelector entry from the DaemonSet. The DaemonSet controller then
creates the pod, kindnet writes the CNI configuration, and the node turns Ready:

   kubectl get pods -n kube-system -l app=kindnet -o wide
   kubectl get nodes
   kubectl get pods -n k8squest -o wide
