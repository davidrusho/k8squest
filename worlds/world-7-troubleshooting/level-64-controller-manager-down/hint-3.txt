🔍 HINT 3: The Fix

In /etc/kubernetes/manifests/kube-controller-manager.yaml (inside the control-plane
node) the image is:

   image: registry.k8s.io/kube-controller-manager:v1.73.0     ❌

The cluster runs Kubernetes v1.37.0. Other control-plane manifests show the right
tag (grep image: /etc/kubernetes/manifests/*.yaml). Correct it and save; the kubelet
recreates the pod by itself.

   kubectl get pods -n kube-system -w
   kubectl get deployment,rs,pods -n k8squest
