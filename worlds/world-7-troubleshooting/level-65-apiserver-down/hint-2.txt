🔍 HINT 2: Read the Container's Own Log

On the control-plane node:

   crictl ps -a --name kube-apiserver
   crictl logs <container-id> 2>&1 | tail -20

The API server stores everything in etcd. The log shows which address it keeps
trying and failing to reach. Compare it with where etcd really listens:

   grep etcd-servers /etc/kubernetes/manifests/kube-apiserver.yaml
   grep listen-client-urls /etc/kubernetes/manifests/etcd.yaml

🤔 Question: do the two ports match?
