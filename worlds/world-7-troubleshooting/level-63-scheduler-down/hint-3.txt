🔍 HINT 3: The Fix

In /etc/kubernetes/manifests/kube-scheduler.yaml (inside the control-plane node)
the command has:

   --kubeconfig=/etc/kubernetes/scheduler.cof     ❌

The file next to it is scheduler.conf. Correct the flag and save. Do not restart
anything by hand: the kubelet notices the changed manifest and recreates the pod.

   kubectl get pods -n kube-system -w
   kubectl get pods -n k8squest -o wide

Do not set nodeName on the Deployment: that bypasses the scheduler and the
validator rejects it.
