🔍 HINT 1: Observing the Problem

1️⃣ Where are the pods?
   kubectl get pods -n k8squest -o wide

2️⃣ Is anything saying why?
   kubectl describe pod -n k8squest -l app=checkout | tail -8

   A pod the scheduler could not place has FailedScheduling events. These have none.

3️⃣ Check the control plane:
   kubectl get pods -n kube-system

🤔 Question: which component assigns pods to nodes, and what is its status?
