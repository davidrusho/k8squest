🔍 HINT 2: Snapshot, Then Verify

   etcdctl --endpoints=https://127.0.0.1:2379 \
     --cacert=/etc/kubernetes/pki/etcd/ca.crt \
     --cert=/etc/kubernetes/pki/etcd/server.crt \
     --key=/etc/kubernetes/pki/etcd/server.key \
     snapshot save /var/lib/etcd/k8squest-snapshot.db

(run it with `kubectl -n kube-system exec etcd-<node> --` in front). Only /var/lib/etcd
and the certificate directory are mounted from the node into the etcd pod, which is
why the snapshot goes under /var/lib/etcd.

Verify it with etcdutl (a restore tool that works on a file, with no server):
   etcdutl snapshot status /var/lib/etcd/k8squest-snapshot.db -w table

Part 2: the node's kubelet is down. Mark the node unschedulable, then evict what is on it:
   kubectl cordon k8squest-multinode-worker
   kubectl drain k8squest-multinode-worker --ignore-daemonsets --delete-emptydir-data --force --timeout=30s
