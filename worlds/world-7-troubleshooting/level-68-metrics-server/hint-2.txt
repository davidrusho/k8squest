🔍 HINT 2: Read the Pod's Log

A pod that is Running but not Ready is failing its readiness check. Ask it why:

   kubectl logs -n kube-system -l k8s-app=metrics-server --tail=20
   kubectl describe pod -n kube-system -l k8s-app=metrics-server | tail -15

metrics-server scrapes every node's kubelet. The log names the problem it hits when
it does. kind's kubelets serve certificates that the cluster CA did not sign.
